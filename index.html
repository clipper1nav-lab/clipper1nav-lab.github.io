Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool that turns gaming livestreams into short vertical clips and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channels as Shorts.

It is used by one person to manage their own channels. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.

What it does with YouTube:

  • uploads Shorts to the channels its owner logs in with, and
  • reads the view counts of those uploads, to see which clips perform well.
