Clipper privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Clipper is a personal tool used by one person to upload short clips to their own YouTube channels. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, Clipper asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish Shorts to the owner's channel.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel name and the view counts of its own uploads.

It does not read email, contacts, files or any other Google data, and it does not access other people's accounts.

How the data is stored

The sign-in tokens and view counts are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps. Deleting the tokens from the owner's computer also stops all access.

Google API Services

Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses the YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen, or open an issue at github.com/clipper1nav-lab/clipper1nav-lab.github.io.
